# Humanities in Tech — Product Strategy Case Study (Public)

## Product Context
Humanities in Tech is a focused product strategy initiative for an AI-assisted CV and cover letter optimiser. It supports humanities and nontraditional candidates who need to translate valuable but differently-signaled experience into credible applications for tech and tech-adjacent roles.

## Problem Statement
Many capable candidates are filtered out not because of a lack of ability, but because their experience is not framed in language that hiring teams expect. The core problem is translation and positioning, not talent scarcity.

## Target User
- Career switchers from humanities backgrounds
- Candidates with nonlinear or nontraditional career paths
- Applicants who need help articulating transferable skills with precision

## MVP Strategy
The MVP is intentionally narrow:
1. Ingest candidate resume data
2. Ingest target job listing context
3. Collect key clarification inputs through follow-up questions
4. Generate practical outputs:
   - Reframed CV draft
   - Role-aligned cover letter draft
   - Missing-information checklist
   - Positioning/gap note

This creates a usable end-to-end workflow without overextending into broad career-coaching features.

## Why the Product Is Focused (Not Broad)
- Focus improves output quality and trust.
- Tight scope enables faster iteration on what matters most to users.
- A clear use case is easier to validate with real candidate outcomes.
- Avoids the dilution common in “do-everything” job-search tools.

## What Was Deliberately Excluded
- Generic all-in-one job platform functionality
- Deep enterprise workflow features
- Broad personality/career testing modules
- Private prompt internals and golden-set methodology details
- Any private codebase implementation details
