# Humanities in Tech — Brand Guide (Public)

## Brand Purpose
Humanities in Tech helps humanities and nontraditional candidates present themselves with clarity and credibility for tech and tech-adjacent opportunities.

## Audience
- Primary: Career transitioners from humanities and adjacent nontraditional backgrounds
- Secondary: Early-career professionals reframing existing experience for product, operations, support, and other tech-adjacent roles
- Tertiary: Collaborators, mentors, and hiring-facing advisors who support candidate storytelling

## Visual Principles
1. **Credible over flashy** — polished, professional, and grounded.
2. **Minimal over cluttered** — strong hierarchy and generous whitespace.
3. **Aspirational over performative** — optimistic and confident, not hype-driven.
4. **Google-adjacent clarity** — familiar, modern, accessible visual language.

## Logo Usage Notes
- Treat the logo as the primary identity anchor across docs and future marketing surfaces.
- Use full-color logo lockups on light neutral backgrounds whenever possible.
- Preserve clear space around the mark (recommended minimum: height of the lowercase “h” in the wordmark).
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects (drop shadows, bevels, glows).
- If logo files are present in `branding/assets/`, they are the visual source of truth.

## Color Usage Notes
Core palette:
- Blue: `#4285F4`
- Red: `#EA4335`
- Yellow: `#FBBC05`
- Green: `#34A853`
- Black: `#111111`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Light background: `#F8F9FA`
- Border gray: `#DADCE0`

Usage guidance:
- Use blue as primary action/accent color.
- Use red/yellow/green as supporting accents and semantic highlights.
- Keep dark text primarily `#111111` for legibility.
- Prefer light, neutral backgrounds and subtle borders.
- Avoid oversaturating a single screen with all four accent colors at once.

## Typography Direction
- Match logo typography style as closely as possible: clean, geometric, legible, and Google-adjacent.
- If exact font matching is unknown, recommended Google Fonts substitutes:
  - **Primary**: Inter, Manrope, or DM Sans
  - **Alternate headline option**: Poppins (with restrained use)
- Prioritize readability and consistency over decorative styling.

## UI Tone
- Calm, competent, and supportive
- Founder-led and human
- Practical, not academic-heavy
- Professional without being corporate-stiff

## What the Brand Should Avoid
- Generic “AI slop” visuals and wording
- Neon-heavy startup aesthetics
- Childish color handling or rainbow overload
- Overpromising outcomes (“guaranteed job” language)
- Dense, jargon-heavy copy blocks
